# Syllo

Syllo turns source material into roadmaps where a learner can plan, study, read, and track progress.

## Language

**Roadmap**:
The learner's editable collection of modules, tasks, resources, notes, review cards, and settings for one goal.
_Avoid_: Workspace, course, project

**Module**:
One skill inside a Roadmap, holding the tasks, notes, and resources that teach it.
_Avoid_: Section, chapter, lesson

**Level**:
One rung of a Roadmap's ladder: Basics, Intermediate, or Advanced. A Level is complete when all its Core Items are done. Roadmaps made before levels existed read as a single Level whose tasks are all core.
_Avoid_: Stage, tier, difficulty

**Current Level**:
The first Level in a Roadmap that is not complete.
_Avoid_: Active level

**Core Item**:
One of at most five tasks per Level that cover the essentials, usually a video. Finishing every Core Item in a Level unlocks level-up.
_Avoid_: Required task, main video

**Go-Deeper Item**:
An optional task in a Level that is not core. It never counts toward level-up.
_Avoid_: Bonus, extra

**Reading Track**:
The books in a Roadmap, kept apart from the Levels so reading never blocks level-up.
_Avoid_: Book list, library

**Focused Roadmap**:
A Roadmap the learner has chosen to work on now. At most three Roadmaps are focused at once; only focused Roadmaps feed Today.
_Avoid_: Active roadmap, pinned roadmap

**Next Step**:
The first unfinished Core Item in a Roadmap's Current Level. Go-Deeper Items and the Reading Track never count.
_Avoid_: Current task, up next

**Today**:
The queue of actions waiting for the learner: resuming a video, review cards due, the Next Step of each Focused Roadmap, Picks to approve, and the next book chapter.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, home feed

**Pick**:
A resource Scout proposes for a goal, waiting for the learner to keep or skip it.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, result

**Learning Session**:
A period in which a learner works in one Roadmap and produces progress, review, timing, or achievement changes.
_Avoid_: Visit, browser session

**Learning Record**:
The durable history of learning activity across Roadmaps, including streaks, achievements, and daily task counts.
_Avoid_: Analytics data, usage data

**Roadmap Intake**:
The conversion of source material and learner choices into a validated Roadmap draft, either from a goal through Scout or from the learner's own notes.
_Avoid_: Parsing, generation
